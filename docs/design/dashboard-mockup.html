What this dashboard does not tell you

  • Why the numbers changed. A drop after v2 can come from the prompt, a change in the alert mix, or a log source that stopped. The POC does not separate these.
  • Whether the agent's claims match the evidence. The POC checks which steps ran, not what the results showed.
  • When to act. There is no baseline and no alert. You read the numbers and decide.
